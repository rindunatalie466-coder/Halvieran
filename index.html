📖 Chapter: Storytelling & Narrative Text

How to Slay Storytelling Like a Pro! ✨

Storytelling itu simpelnya lo lagi "spill the tea" versi Bahasa Inggris yang ada alur dan plot serunya. Biar audiens lo nggak *bored to death*, pahami kuncinya di bawah ini!

1

Orientation

"Intro to the Squad & Vibe"

Kenalin siapa tokoh utamanya (Who), di mana kejadiannya (Where), dan kapan (When). Set the mood dari awal!

Ex: "Once upon a time in a peaceful village, lived a chill guy named Malin..."
2

Complication

"The Drama Hits!"

Mulai muncul masalah atau konflik puncak. Ini bagian paling *spicy* yang bikin penonton penasaran!

Ex: "Suddenly, Malin pretended he didn't know his mom. Totally toxic behavior!"
3

Resolution

"Plot Twist / Solusi"

Bagaimana masalah tersebut diselesaikan. Boleh *happy ending* atau malah *sad ending* (karma berlaku).

Ex: "His mom prayed, storm came, and BOOM! Malin turned into stone."
4

Coda (Moral)

"Pesan Biar Gak Karma"

Pelajaran penting yang bisa diambil audiens. Wajib disampaikan dengan intonasi mantap!

Ex: "Moral lesson: Always respect your parents, no matter how successful you are!"

Cheat Sheet Trik Bahasa (Language Features)

1. Simple Past Tense Gunakan V2! (e.g., went, walked, said, cried)
2. Action Verbs Kata kerja fisik (e.g., jumped, screamed, ran)
3. Time Connectors Penyambung alur (e.g., Suddenly, After that, Finally)
